This application is a despatch application where independent riders can register and get assigned pick up and delivery jobs. It's built with Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL.

<!-- AUTHENTICATION -->

A rider

1. Registers on the platform,
2. Logs in to the platform

<!-- ONBOARDING -->

The rider is expected to fill a form which involves

1. The type of vehicle they use for delivery
2. The type of goods they deliver
3. Upload a picture of themselves
4. Upload a picture of their vehicle
5. Upload a picture of their ID
6. Upload a picture of their driver's license
7. Enter their plate number
8. Enter the model of their vehicle
